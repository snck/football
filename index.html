How to play

On a touchscreen: tap a player or a spot to select it and see the odds. Tap it again, or press Commit, to play it. Cancel clears your choice. The action buttons replace the number keys.

The beat

Every turn is a short passage of play. First the side without the ball sets its trap. Then the side with the ball sees exactly where everyone is and chooses one action. Then it plays out. You always have unlimited time. Nothing is timed.

With the ball (you attack left → rightup the screen)

  • 1 Pass: click a teammate. The % badges show the odds.
  • 2 Through ball: click space ahead of a runner. They race the defenders to it. Aim behind defenders: one already goal-side of the spot doesn't have to turn and gets there sooner.
  • 3 Lofted / Cross: click a teammate or a spot. It goes over the lines but is contested in the air. Crosses into the box become headers.
  • 4 Dribble: click inside the ring. Defenders near your path may tackle.
  • 5 Shoot: the goal chance is shown before you commit. Press 5 again (or tap the buttons) for a driven shot (better from range, blocked more) or a chip (only over a keeper off his line).
  • 6 Clear: hoof it long when you are in trouble.

Click once to preview, then click again or press Enter to commit. Esc cancels.

Quick play: after a completed pass you may play first-time, before the defence resets. It is 5% less accurate (3% after a short pass to a free teammate) and you can't dribble. Or press Space to take a touch and let the defence reorganise.

Runs: before you take that touch you can call a run: B in behind, O overlap or S come short, then click the runner. He makes it next turn, before the defence decides, so they can mark him or try to catch him offside. A through ball to a player who just ran in behind gets a 0.25 s head start.

Counter-attack: when you win the ball with a tackle or an interception, you get one first-time move before the opponent can get back. The CPU can do the same to you.

Corners: cross to the near or far post, or play it short to the teammate near the flag. Last attack: when time is up during an attack in the final third, play goes on until it's over (3 turns at most).

Without the ball

  • 1 Press: click one of your players inside the big ring. They close the carrier down, which makes every option harder for them, but leaves a gap behind.
  • 2 Tackle: click a player inside the small ring to lunge now. You might win it, give a foul (or a penalty in the box), or get beaten.
  • 3 Mark: click a dangerous opponent. Your nearest player tight-marks them and follows them for 2 more turns, until you win the ball or play stops. He leaves his zone and won't cut out balls to anyone else. One mark at a time; X releases it. The red rings show who the CPU wants to find.
  • 4 Cover: click one of your players inside the big ring. They stand off the carrier, then step up to 4 m towards whoever receives the next ball, before it can be played first-time. They're harder to dribble past and foul less, but put little pressure on the pass.
  • T Offside trap: your back line steps up (not a marker). It catches runners on the line, but whoever stepped up is slower to chase a ball in behind.
  • Line height (Q/W/E): deep, normal or high. This setting stays until you change it. Deep protects the box (more blocks, more headers won, fewer balls in behind) but your counters start far from goal. High wins the ball back higher, but balls over the top are dangerous.

Enter confirms your plan. Space just holds shape. How you defend corners (man, zonal or two up) is set in the menu (Esc) and stays.

Reading the pitch

  • Green badges are safe, yellow is a gamble, red is a long shot.
  • Ringed defenders are the ones who threaten your chosen action.
  • The dashed line is the offside line.
  • After each move, the caption explains what happened and what the odds were.
  • Each club has three key players (on the team cards): strong in the air, long passer or dribbler. The preview says when one changes the odds.
  • At half-time you can change your shape for the second half.
