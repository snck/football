How to play

The beat

Every turn is a short passage of play. First the side without the ball sets its trap. Then the side with the ball sees exactly where everyone is and chooses one action. Then it plays out. You always have unlimited time. Nothing is timed.

With the ball (you attack left → right)

  • 1 Pass: click a teammate. The % badges show the odds.
  • 2 Through ball: click space ahead of a runner. They race the defenders to it.
  • 3 Lofted / Cross: click a teammate or a spot. It goes over the lines but is contested in the air. Crosses into the box become headers.
  • 4 Dribble: click inside the ring. Defenders near your path may tackle.
  • 5 Shoot: the goal chance is shown before you commit.
  • 6 Clear: hoof it long when you are in trouble.

Click once to preview, then click again or press Enter to commit. Esc cancels.

Quick play: after a completed pass you may play first-time, before the defence resets. It is 5% less accurate and you can't dribble. Or press Space to take a touch and let the defence reorganise.

Without the ball

  • 1 Press: click one of your players inside the big ring. They close the carrier down, which makes every option harder for them, but leaves a gap behind.
  • 2 Tackle: click a player inside the small ring to lunge now. You might win it, give a foul (or a penalty in the box), or get beaten.
  • 3 Mark: click a dangerous opponent. Your nearest player tight-marks them. The red rings show who the CPU wants to find.
  • T Offside trap: your back line steps up. It catches runners on the line but leaves space in behind.
  • Line height (Q/W/E): deep, normal or high. This setting stays until you change it.

Enter confirms your plan. Space just holds shape.

Reading the pitch

  • Green badges are safe, yellow is a gamble, red is a long shot.
  • Ringed defenders are the ones who threaten your chosen action.
  • The dashed line is the offside line.
  • After each move, the caption explains what happened and what the odds were.
